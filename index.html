    How this works and what to know

    This page uses open-source libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, JSZip and Papa Parse) that run entirely in your browser. Your PDF, the details and the passwords stay in this tab and are cleared when you close or reload it.

    • Recipients need the password to open their file. Most PDF readers support AES-256, including Adobe Acrobat Reader, Chrome, Edge, and the built-in readers on iPhone and Android.
    • Names (after "Bill To" or "Attention"), services (after "Service:" or "Programme:"), invoice numbers, months (from "Service Period" or similar, otherwise the invoice date), amounts (from the "Total" line) and email addresses are read from the PDF's text. Email addresses that appear on most invoices are treated as your own and skipped. Always check the details before sending, especially highlighted fields. Scanned PDFs have no readable text, so details need to be typed in.
    • Your settings, like the email message and file name format, are remembered in this browser only. PDFs, names, email addresses and passwords are never saved. Use Forget my settings under Customise to clear them.
    • CSV files can have the columns name, email, invoice, amount, pages and password. Only pages is required.
    • Email with PDF creates an email file with the locked PDF attached. Classic Outlook for Windows and Apple Mail open it as a new message ready to send. Other email apps may open it as a received message instead, so test one first.
    • The tool never sends email itself. You review and send each message from your own email app.
    • File names follow the format you choose, with characters that aren't allowed in file names removed. If two files would get the same name, a number is added to the second.
    • Avoid passwords that others could easily know or guess, such as ID numbers or birthdays.
    • Fillable form fields and some interactive features in the original PDF may not carry over to the split files.
